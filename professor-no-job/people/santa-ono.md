# Santa J. Ono

Case role: former president, University of Michigan.

## What the record shows

Ono's career is heavily academic—Johns Hopkins, Harvard, University College London, Emory, Cincinnati, UBC, and Michigan—but he is **not** an academia-only case.

University of Michigan materials state that he:

- advised GSK, Johnson & Johnson, Merck, and Novartis;
- served as director and chief scientific officer of iCo Therapeutics.

A University of Cincinnati profile also describes him as helping start iCo Therapeutics and serving as its chief scientific officer.
