# Pamela Whitten

Current case role: President, Indiana University.

## What the record shows

Indiana University's official timeline is overwhelmingly academic after the mid-1990s: University of Kansas Medical Center, Michigan State, Purdue, Georgia, Kennesaw State, then Indiana University.

However, this is **not** an academia-only case. Profiles from the University of Kentucky and Indianapolis Business Journal say that after her 1986 master's degree she worked in corporate communications before returning for doctoral study. IBJ specifies corporate communications in Germany and health-care communications in the United States.
