# Rose B. Bellanca

Current case role: President, Washtenaw Community College.

## Correction to the initial hypothesis

The record supports the memory that Bellanca taught home economics/vocational subjects, but **not** the claim that this was her only previous job.

A 1976 *Grosse Pointe News* staff listing records Rose Bellanca as a Home Economics teacher at Grosse Pointe North High School. WCC's 2023–24 faculty guide contains Bellanca's own statement that early in her career she was a vocational education teacher teaching culinary arts to high-school students.

The Congressional Record gives a longer chronology:

- began teaching in 1973 at Fitzgerald High School;
- nine years later became director of vocational-technical education in Macomb County while working for Chippewa Valley School District;
- moved into Macomb Community College administration as assistant to the president, interim vice president, vice president for planning and development, and provost.

WCC's biography then lists later presidencies at Northwood University's West Palm Beach campus and St. Clair County Community College before WCC.
