# Erhan Bayraktar

Current case role: Susan M. Smith Professor of Mathematics and director of the University of Michigan M.S. Program in Quantitative Finance and Risk Management.

Cross-links: `finance`, `quants`, `grants`.

## Why this is an important case

This case tests something more specific than "has the professor ever held a nonacademic job?"

The relevant domain-practice question is:

> Has the person directing a professional master's program in quantitative finance and risk management actually worked in quantitative finance, banking, trading, asset management, insurance/risk management, or another finance-industry role?

Bayraktar's published CV lists:

- Princeton Ph.D., 2004;
- U-M T. H. Hildebrandt Research Assistant Professor, 2004–2006;
- U-M Assistant Professor, 2006–2010;
- U-M Associate Professor, 2010–2013;
- U-M Professor, 2013–present;
- Director of the Quantitative Finance and Risk Management master's program, 2015–present.

No bank, hedge fund, trading firm, asset manager, insurer, risk-management company, or other finance-industry employer is listed in the CV's employment history.

That does **not** prove he has never consulted, traded, advised a firm, or undertaken other unlisted outside work. It supports the narrower and directly checkable statement:

> His published employment history contains no finance-industry job.
