# Cornell Systems Engineering M.Eng. — Mechatronic & Robotics Systems

## Product

Cornell Systems Engineering allows M.Eng. students to create a pathway called **Mechatronic & Robotics Systems**.

The on-campus M.Eng. is a one-year professional degree. Cornell Engineering's general M.Eng. page markets the credential as a way to **accelerate your career**, emphasizes real-world projects, professional development, employer connections, and lists major employers of M.Eng. graduates.

The Systems Engineering admissions material says the M.Eng. is **self-funded**.
