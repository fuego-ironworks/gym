# Berkeley Haas Master of Financial Engineering

## Marketing

The current Berkeley Haas MFE site uses strong employment-oriented language, including descriptions of unusually strong placement, employer connections, and personalized career services.

Official pages:
- https://mfe.haas.berkeley.edu/
- https://mfe.haas.berkeley.edu/careers
- https://mfe.haas.berkeley.edu/careers/mfe-placements
- https://mfe.haas.berkeley.edu/admissions/faq

## Explicit non-guarantee

The FAQ asks whether internships are guaranteed and answers that **it is not possible to guarantee each student an internship**. Students are expected to work actively with MFE staff. An internship or approved on-site project is nevertheless required for graduation.

That is unusually useful evidence because the program itself distinguishes intensive placement assistance from a guarantee.

## Full-time outcome

For the Class of 2025 the program reports:

- 70 students looking for jobs;
- 67 students receiving offers;
- 67 accepting offers.

The program therefore reports very high placement, but not universal placement among job seekers.

No explicit guarantee of a full-time post-graduation job was found on the official pages reviewed on 2026-09-27.

## Training distinction

Preserve all three statements simultaneously:

1. Haas markets career placement aggressively.
2. Haas reports strong historical employment outcomes.
3. Neither of those facts is the same as a contractual promise that each graduate will receive a job.

That distinction is the purpose of this branch.
